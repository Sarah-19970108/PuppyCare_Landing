For owner-run dog daycares in Ireland

Say goodbye to scattered paper records, one simple system for your daycare’s bookings and vaccination checks.

PuppyCare is made for daycares that still take bookings by text, social media, phone and in person, and keep paper records.

The problem, in a daycare owner’s words

“We struggle to keep track of how many dogs are due in on any given day. Medical records are on paper, so they’re easily overlooked, and if the handwriting is hard to read, a dog whose vaccines aren’t up to date could slip through and infect others.”

How it works

  1. Book a spotPick a day for a dog. PuppyCare checks there is room and that the vaccinations are in date, and offers the nearest day with room if the day is full.
  2. Check inVaccination status shows at a glance when the dog arrives: amber when one expires within 14 days, red when one has expired, and an expired dog cannot be checked in.
  3. Get updatesStaff tap once to log what each dog did (ate, walked, played, napped), and owners see their own dog’s day on Pupdate instead of calling or messaging.

What works today

This is what it does

  • Bookings with a daily limit of 40 places and a vaccination check
  • The nearest day with room offered when a day is full
  • Red and amber vaccination warnings, with check-in blocked for red
  • One-tap notes: ate, walked, played, napped
  • Pupdate: an owner sees only their own dog’s day
